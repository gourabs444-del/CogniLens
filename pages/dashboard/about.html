About CogniLens

Behavioral analysis for clearer self-awareness, decision patterns, and personality insight.

CogniLens is a behavioral analysis platform designed to help individuals understand their thinking patterns, decision-making style, and personality tendencies.

Our system uses multiple phases such as baseline behavior, resource prioritization, moral scenarios, time-pressure responses, and consistency checks to provide deeper and more realistic insights compared to traditional personality tests.

The results generated by CogniLens are intended for self-awareness and personal growth. They are not a substitute for professional psychological, medical, or clinical advice.

We are committed to maintaining user privacy and ensuring that personal data is handled responsibly. Our goal is to create a reliable and insightful platform that helps users better understand themselves in a structured and meaningful way.

CogniLens aims to go beyond basic quizzes and deliver a more advanced, data-driven personality experience.
