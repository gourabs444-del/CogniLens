Help & Guide

Quick guidance for taking the assessment, reading your result, and fixing common issues.

How does CogniLens work?

CogniLens analyzes your personality through a multi-phase assessment system. Each phase is designed to capture different aspects of your thinking and behavior, including decision-making, priorities, reactions under pressure, and consistency.

Your responses are processed using a weighted scoring system to generate a personality type along with deeper behavioral insights.

How should I take the test?

Answer honestly based on what you naturally do, not what you think is "correct."

Some questions may feel similar or repeated. This is intentional and helps improve accuracy by checking consistency.

In time-limited questions, respond quickly to capture your natural reaction.

How should I understand my results?

Your result represents your general personality tendencies based on your responses.

It includes your personality type, behavioral patterns, and decision style. These insights are meant for self-awareness and personal growth.

Results may vary over time as your thinking and experiences evolve.

How accurate is this test?

CogniLens uses a structured and multi-layered approach to improve accuracy compared to basic personality quizzes.

However, it is not a professional psychological or medical assessment and should not be treated as a definitive diagnosis.

Common issues

  • If the test is not progressing, ensure you have selected an option before continuing.
  • In allocation questions, make sure the total points equal 10.
  • For the best experience, use a stable internet connection.
